Open Schedule — Staff Guide

Quick reference guide for team members and staff

1 Logging In

To access your personal schedule and timeclock on Open Schedule:

  • If you received a welcome link: Click the link from your manager. It will automatically connect to your organization's cloud server.
  • If connecting manually: Enter the Cloud Server URL and Public Key provided by your manager. Once saved, your browser will remember the server.
  • Enter your username & password: Type your assigned employee username and password to log in directly into Employee View.

2 Viewing Your Schedule

Once logged in, your personalized mobile dashboard displays:

  • Upcoming Shifts: View your assigned dates, start and end times, department, and role.
  • Shift Sync: Add your shifts to your phone's personal calendar (iCal / Google Calendar) with one tap.

3 Clocking In & Out

You can record your punches depending on your workplace configuration:

  • Smartphone Mobile Clock-In: Tap Clock In when you are inside the workplace geofence.
  • 2-Minute Kiosk Check-In Code: If you are outside the geofence or GPS is unavailable, enter the 2-minute rolling code displayed on your workplace's shared Kiosk tablet.
  • Shared Kiosk Tablet: You can also clock in directly on the breakroom Kiosk tablet using your 4-digit PIN.

4 Shift Swaps & Open Shifts

Need time off or extra hours?

  • Up for Grabs: Post an upcoming shift if you need a qualified coworker to cover it.
  • Claiming Shifts: Browse open shifts posted by coworkers and claim them to add them to your schedule.

5 Availability & Time Off

  • Availability: Submit your recurring weekly preferred and unavailable hours so managers schedule you when you're free.
  • Time-Off Requests: Request specific days off in advance. You'll receive a confirmation when approved by management.
🔒 Privacy & Security: Your password and personal details are protected by local encryption in your browser. Never share your password or Kiosk PIN with others.
